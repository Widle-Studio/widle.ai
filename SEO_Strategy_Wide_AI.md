# Comprehensive SEO Strategy & Ranking Guide for Wide.ai

This document outlines the SEO ranking factors and a step-by-step implementation plan for **wide.ai**, a service agency specializing in AI consulting, custom LLM development, machine learning solutions, and workflow automation for global B2B companies.

---

## Part 1: Key Ranking Factors for AI & Tech Agencies

To rank successfully in the highly competitive B2B AI space, search engines (primarily Google) look for trust, authority, and relevance.

### 1. Topical Authority (E-E-A-T)
Google evaluates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness.
- **Expertise:** Deep, technical content written by actual practitioners (AI engineers, data scientists).
- **Authoritativeness:** Earning backlinks from reputable tech publications, industry associations, and academic institutions.
- **Trustworthiness:** Transparent "About Us" pages, clear contact information, client testimonials, and case studies.

### 2. Technical SEO Foundations
A modern AI agency website must perform flawlessly.
- **Core Web Vitals:** Fast loading speeds, visual stability, and quick interactivity (especially critical if using Next.js/React).
- **Mobile Friendliness:** Seamless experience on mobile devices.
- **Crawlability & Indexability:** Clean XML sitemaps, proper `robots.txt`, and sensible URL structures.
- **Security:** HTTPS encryption is mandatory.

### 3. On-Page Relevance & Optimization
- **Search Intent:** Does your page solve the specific problem the user is searching for? (e.g., "how to implement LLM" vs "hire custom LLM agency").
- **Title Tags & Meta Descriptions:** Optimized with primary keywords to drive click-through rates (CTR).
- **Heading Structure (H1, H2, H3):** Logical content hierarchy using semantic HTML.
- **Internal Linking:** Connecting related topics together (e.g., linking your "AI Consulting" page to a blog post about "AI Readiness Audits").

### 4. Backlinks and Off-Page Signals
- **Quality over Quantity:** A link from TechCrunch or a recognized AI directory is worth infinitely more than 100 low-quality links.
- **Digital PR:** Publishing novel AI research, open-source tools, or unique data sets that naturally attract links.

## Part 2: SEO Implementation Roadmap

Follow this phased approach to implement the strategy.

### Phase 1: Technical & Foundation Setup (Months 1)
1. **Analytics Setup:** Configure Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), and conversion tracking (form submissions, calls).
2. **Site Architecture:** Structure the site logically:
   - `/services/ai-consulting`
   - `/services/custom-llm-development`
   - `/services/workflow-automation`
   - `/case-studies/...`
   - `/blog/...`
3. **Technical Audit:** Ensure fast Next.js rendering (SSR/SSG), optimized images, and valid schema markup (Organization and Service schema).

### Phase 2: Keyword Research & Core Page Optimization (Month 2)
1. **Keyword Mapping:** Identify high-intent bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) keywords.
   - *Example:* "Enterprise AI consulting firm", "Custom LLM development agency"
2. **Landing Page Optimization:** Update Title tags, Meta descriptions, and H1s on all core service pages.
3. **Copywriting:** Ensure service pages have 800+ words of highly relevant, jargon-appropriate text detailing the process, tech stack, and outcomes. Include clear Call to Actions (CTAs).

### Phase 3: Content Strategy & Topical Authority (Months 3 - 6)
1. **Create a Content Hub:** Launch an educational blog or "Resource Center".
2. **Middle-of-Funnel (MOFU) Content:** Address specific industry pain points.
   - *Example Idea:* "How to prepare your company's data for a Custom LLM"
   - *Example Idea:* "Top 5 AI Automation Use Cases for FinTech"
3. **Case Studies:** Publish detailed case studies formatted as Problem -> Solution -> Tech Stack -> Results. Search engines love data-backed results.

### Phase 4: Off-Page SEO & Link Building (Month 4 & Ongoing)
1. **Industry Directories:** Submit wide.ai to software development and AI agency directories (e.g., Clutch, G2, specialized AI lists).
2. **Guest Posting / Digital PR:** Have your founders or lead engineers write technical articles for platforms like Towards Data Science, HackerNoon, or industry-specific magazines.
3. **Open Source/Tools:** Release a free micro-tool or an open-source repo on GitHub linked back to wide.ai. This is a massive link magnet in the tech space.
